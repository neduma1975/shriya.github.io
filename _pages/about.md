I am a first-year **Mechanical Engineering** student at **Caltech (Class of 2030)** focused on human-centered hardware: soft robotics, rehabilitation instrumentation, wearables, and autonomous robotic systems. Seeking **Summer 2027 internships** in mechanical design, prototyping, manufacturing, and robotics R&D.

## Research & Engineering Experience

- **Gao Research Group / FSRI (Caltech)**: Co-developed a wireless transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation (taVNS) + BLE audio earbud (<$200 BOM) with KiCad PCB layout, Phrozen 16K SLA resin housings, and machined aluminum electrodes (0.9 Ω vs. 165.0 kΩ graphite-silicone composite, n=6).
- **NeuroRobotics Lab (University of Utah)**: Executed 3 PCB iterations and mechanical/sensor integration for an instrumented stroke-rehab utensil handle (115 × 33 mm, <80 g, 0–100 N grip force, Python force/IMU pipeline). Presented at the CNI Symposium & Utah Undergraduate Research Symposium.
- **SJSU ARAS Lab**: Designed a 2-iteration DJI Matrice belly mount for a 1.5 kg MicroHSI 410 hyperspectral camera in SolidWorks with linear static FEA (20 N load, 1.59 MPa max plate stress, 0.015 mm max displacement) and NDVI mapping.
- **Purdue Wachs Lab**: Designed compact 3D-printed camera-control supernumerary robotic limb hardware and hybrid intention-prediction pipeline in Python and C++.
